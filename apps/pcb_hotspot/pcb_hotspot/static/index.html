What would fix it?

Re-solves the board with common fixes — thermal vias under the critical part, heavier copper, more planes, airflow, chassis clamping — and shows how many degrees each one buys.

Measurements

One per line. Board points: x_mm, y_mm, T_°C[, top|bottom] (thermocouple or IR spot). Junction readings: ComponentName, T_°C (on-die sensor). With 5+ points, 20% are held out to test the calibrated model.

Fits two physical unknowns with PINNeAPPle's Ensemble Kalman Inversion: the real surface cooling (enclosure, nearby parts) and the real in-plane spreading (copper coverage/quality).

Make the model match your board

Correlation-based models are typically within ±20%. A few measured temperatures pin down the actual cooling and copper spreading of your product, and the junction temperatures are re-predicted with their uncertainty.
